How a session runs

  1. Groups of four or five, one per table. Six tables, one prompt each.
  2. Four minutes talk, two minutes writing on the session page.
  3. Groups move up one table. Six rotations, 36 minutes.
  4. Table 1 is the teacher table. Every group comes to you once.

60 minutes
Starter 5 · Table talk 8 · model at Table 1 2 · rotations 36 · review 5

The booklet

  • One per student, kept for all three sessions. Print landscape, double-sided.
  • Front page: name, rubric, Table 1 record (teacher only).
  • Session pages: one row per table, Tables 2 to 6.

Speaking Folio booklet (Word)

Making groups

  1. Open the Group shuffler on the classroom computer. Groups save in that browser.
  2. Paste the class list, one name per line. Keep-apart pairs as Sam / Alex.
  3. Shuffle all sessions: six groups for each session, different company each time.
  4. Move someone: click the name, then the group. Or Edit as text.
  5. Display mode on the Prompts page shows who is at each table.

On the screen

  • Table talk (1 · 2 · 3): Next shows a line, Next again marks it against the rubric. − + change the level. The lesson plan says which lines to stop on.
  • Prompts page, display mode (1 · 2 · 3): prompts, who is at each table, the talk / write clock. Space starts and pauses.
  • Rubric for the review.

Table 1

  • Front pages of the group's booklets in front of you. Listen; hand a turn to the quiet one.
  • Record a level per strand and the words you heard.
  • Level 9 is Year 9 expected. Listening 10 and 11 are the extension.
  • Presenting: Session 2 (the Reader) and Session 3 (the sentence to the class).
Listen for

1: a feeling with a because; a question that gets a reason; a named person answered.

2: a side with a reason from the book; the opponent quoted back; a reframe; a concession; the Reader staying neutral.

3: a feature located in words; a "yes, and"; a point taken further; turns given by name; one clear sentence.

Assessment

  • Assessment Task 1 of the unit. The front page is the record.
  • Session pages are the Listening evidence. Dot points are fine.
  • Report the level shown consistently across the three sessions.

EAL and support

  • Pre-teach the sentence starters (Prompts page) as a script.
  • Table talk at level 7 first.
  • A minute of first-language planning at each table. The talk is in English.
  • Reader role in Session 2 at a table already heard once.
