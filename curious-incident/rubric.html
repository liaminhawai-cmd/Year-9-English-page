Where the evidence comes from

What the teacher listens for at Table 1

One session, one group at a time. The moves are the four colours on the WAGOLL wall.

Session 1 · Personal response

  • Says how the quote hit them, with a because · Interacting 5 to 7
  • Asks what someone meant before answering · Listening 5 to 7
  • Goes back to the words when they respond · Listening 8
  • Notices who hasn't spoken · Interacting 10

Session 2 · I couldn't disagree more

  • Reader: reads clearly, then runs the talk without taking over · Presenting 6 to 8, Interacting 8
  • Takes a side and gives a reason from the book · Interacting 5
  • Answers the other person's actual reason · Listening 8 to 9
  • Changes tone between persuading and conceding · Interacting 9

Session 3 · Passage panel

  • Names a writing feature and the words it lives in · Interacting 7
  • Refuses a vague word and asks for the evidence · Listening 9
  • Turns an observation into a claim about effect · Interacting 7
  • Share-back: one clear sentence to the class · Presenting 8 to 11
