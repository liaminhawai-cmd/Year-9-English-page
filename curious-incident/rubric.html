What the teacher listens for at Table 1

One session, one group at a time. The moves are the ones on the Table talk page.

Session 1 · Personal response

  • Feeling with a reason, from the words on the table · Interacting 5 and 7
  • Link to their own life that comes back to the text · Interacting 7
  • Link to a named person's idea · Listening 7, Interacting 7
  • A question that gets a reason out of someone, or infers what they imply · Listening 5 and 8
  • Notices who hasn't spoken · Interacting 10

Session 2 · I couldn't disagree more

  • A side with a reason from the book · Interacting 5
  • Quotes the opponent's exact words before answering · Listening 8 and 9
  • Reframes the statement into the real question · Listening 10
  • Concedes and keeps the rest of the argument · Interacting 9
  • Reader: reads, runs it, takes no side · Presenting 6, Interacting 8

Session 3 · Passage panel

  • Feature located in words on the page · Interacting 7
  • 'Yes, and' that adds · Listening 11
  • Takes someone else's point further · Listening 11 and 12
  • Turns given by name; first speaker returned to · Interacting 10
  • One clear sentence for the class · Presenting 8
