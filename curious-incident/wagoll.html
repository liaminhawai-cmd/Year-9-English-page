Same table, same quote, three levels

“I do not tell lies. Mother used to say that this was because I was a good person. But it is not because I am a good person. It is because I can't tell lies.”Session 1 · Table 4 · Chapter 37
The prompt asks how the quote hit you, whether never lying is a strength or a problem, and whether a kind lie exists. Four people, five minutes. Each column is a different table having the same five minutes.

The other two sessions, at the expected level, and one that isn't working

The marks are a taxonomy, not a score: a line can carry two moves, and a great five minutes does not need every colour in every line. What separates the columns is whether reasons are given, whether words from the book are pointed at, whether anyone checks what they heard, and whether the quiet person gets asked. Continuum lines are from the school's Speaking and Listening continuum; see the rubric.
